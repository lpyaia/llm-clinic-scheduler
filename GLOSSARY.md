# Clinic scheduling

Patients search for available appointments and refine their preferences across a conversation.

## Language

**Appointment query**:
A patient's request to list their own appointments, optionally filtered by status and date interval.

**Search preferences**:
The patient's current choices of doctor, specialty, period of day and dates for an availability search.
_Avoid_: Search parameters, search settings

**Selected interval**:
The date interval chosen by the patient before a weekday refinement narrows its start.

**Weekday refinement**:
A preference for dates after a named weekday.
It narrows the selected interval when one exists; replacing it can move the search earlier within that interval.
